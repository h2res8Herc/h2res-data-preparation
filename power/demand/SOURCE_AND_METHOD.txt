Electricity-demand sources and method
=====================================

National hourly magnitude
-------------------------
ENTSO-E Transparency Platform, Actual Total Load.
API documentType=A65 and processType=A16.
https://transparency.entsoe.eu/

Spatial weights
---------------
European Commission Joint Research Centre, EU energy atlas - energy demand
2019, electricity_tot_demand_2019.tif.
https://data.jrc.ec.europa.eu/dataset/76a6b550-253c-44a4-9a4c-d22079e7bf62
DOI: 10.2905/JRC.XDKBE3R

The original 1 x 1 km raster is expressed in toe per cell. It was summed inside
NUTS 2021 level-3 polygons and normalized independently within each country.
Only the normalized spatial shares are packaged. The selected ENTSO-E year sets
the hourly and annual magnitude.

The generated NUTS3 values sum exactly to national ENTSO-E load in every hour.
Model-zone values are sums of the corresponding NUTS3 values.
